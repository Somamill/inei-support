# inei. support and privacy

inei. is an iPhone and iPad camera and photo editor. Save photos and reversible edits in Apple Photos, use Auto Edit on an individual photo or a selection, and compare presets before applying them.

## Support

Questions, ideas, or a bug report? [Email Max Miller](mailto:max@miller-max.com). If you report a problem, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and how to reproduce it. Share only images or details you are comfortable sending.

If the Library is empty, check your Photos permission for inei. in Settings. With limited Photos access, you can add more photos to the selection in iOS or iPadOS. An original stored in iCloud Photos may need time to download.

## Privacy policy

inei. processes photos on your device. The app does not require an account and does not send your photos, edits, photo grades, or location to the developer. This release has no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

- **Apple Photos.** The app uses the Photos permissions you grant to browse images and save photos and edits. Apple manages iCloud Photos when you enable it on your device. inei. requests cloud photos only on unmetered Wi-Fi or Ethernet.
- **Camera and location.** The app uses the camera when you open the camera view. If you enable geotagging and grant location permission, the capture location is added to the photo's metadata. A photo you share may include location or other metadata.
- **On-device preferences.** inei. stores preferences, photo tags, and grades on your device. You can review camera, photo, and location permissions in system Settings.
- **Apple Maps and iCloud Journey.** If you request a place lookup, the app sends the place query or the existing coordinates you ask it to look up to Apple Maps. Text recognition runs on the device. When iCloud is available, Journey syncs progress totals and completed walks through your Apple account. Individual grades, photos, and walk starting coordinates stay out of the Journey sync.
- **Sharing and feedback.** You choose the destination when you share an image, and can choose Share Without Metadata. If you email support, the developer receives the email and any information you choose to include.

Contact [max@miller-max.com](mailto:max@miller-max.com) with privacy questions.
